Before You Book

Questions we hear all the time

Most accent wraps take one to two days. Full car and SUV wraps typically take several days depending on the vehicle, film, and level of detail. We will give you a clear timeline with your quote.
We wrap most makes, models, and body styles. Send us your vehicle details and the look you have in mind so we can confirm the best film and installation approach.
When installed and removed properly, quality wrap film is designed to protect the original paint beneath it. We inspect the surface before installation and talk through any existing damage before we begin.
We provide care instructions at pickup. Gentle hand washing, wrap-safe products, and keeping harsh chemicals away from the edges will help your finish stay sharp.
Tell us about your vehicle, the finish or graphics you want, and your timing. We will talk through options, answer questions, and prepare a straightforward quote for your build.
